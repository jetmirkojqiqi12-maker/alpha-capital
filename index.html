The most common questions traders ask before claiming Premium.

Why is futures trading so popular?

There isn't one. We removed the daily kill-switch entirely. Your only constraint is the Maximum Loss Limit (MLL), which trails your equity and never resets after your first payout.

How fast are payouts?

Same-day on request. Submit your withdrawal in the dashboard and our team processes it within hours during business days.

Which platforms can I trade on?

Premium is supported across NinjaTrader, Tradovate, Quantower, CQG and Deepchart. You can switch any time at no cost.

What's the difference between With and Without Activation?

With Activation: lower monthly with a one-time $149 activation fee paid only when you qualify. Without Activation: slightly higher monthly, $0 activation. Same rules either way.

What happens after I get qualified?

The 50% consistency rule is dropped, the profit target is removed, and you can request payouts after 5 green trading days. Your MLL holds — it doesn't reset.

Are these real-money or simulated accounts?

All Alpha Futures accounts are simulated/demo, in line with the futures prop industry. Performance fees are paid in real currency from the firm.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Premium is a monthly subscription, cancel from your dashboard. The launch 25% off discount stays applied for as long as your subscription is active.
